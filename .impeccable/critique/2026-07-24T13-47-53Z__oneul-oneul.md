## P0

None found.

## P1

1. Restore Today context and remove top clipping. Show the selected month/day/weekday in the compact title. Remove the `-7` hour-label offset or add matching top padding. Evidence: `Features/Today/TodayView.swift:287`, `Features/Today/DayGridView.swift:177`.
2. Repair primary-state contrast. Give the AI input a persistent high-contrast label or shorter example, and use an accent color that remains visible in light mode. Evidence: `Features/AI/AIScheduleView.swift:106`, `Design/Theme.swift:27`.
3. Complete the accessibility contract. Add explicit labels to the AI send button and hidden-label pickers, make date/weekday targets at least 44pt, and stop repeating AI motion when Reduce Motion is enabled. Evidence: `Features/AI/AIScheduleView.swift:141`, `Features/AI/AIScheduleView.swift:632`, `Features/Editor/EventEditorView.swift:72`, `Features/Today/CalendarBar.swift:169`.
4. Keep the floating add button out of schedule content. Add a bottom content inset equal to the button footprint or move it into a toolbar/safe-area region. Evidence: `Features/Today/TodayView.swift:81`, `Features/Today/TodayView.swift:189`.
5. Simplify schedule manipulation. Prefer one tap to edit and long press to move; keep resizing available in the editor. Add confirmation or Undo for destructive single deletion. Evidence: `Features/Today/DayGridView.swift:321`, `Features/Editor/EventEditorView.swift:177`.

## P2

- Reduce glass-card repetition in Settings and EventEditor; reserve strong glass treatments for app chrome and important results.
- Standardize navigation-title behavior across Today, Memo, AI, and Settings.
- Add an accessibility-size layout path for fixed-height time blocks instead of forcing full metadata into short events.

## Strengths

- The time grid, event height, title hierarchy, and rainbow sequence make the core calendar immediately scannable.
- The top-level navigation uses native iOS patterns: four primary tabs plus a system search role.
- Event blocks already expose VoiceOver edit/delete actions, and AI results are reviewable and editable before persistence.
- Memo search and deletion Undo use familiar system behavior.
- The current source removes the microphone control by request; the older supplied AI screenshot is not treated as a current-design defect.

## Adjudication

Independent scores were 69 and 74. The final 72 excludes the already-resolved microphone screenshot/source mismatch, retains deductions reproduced by both reviewers, and gives the accessibility category partial credit for the existing event-block VoiceOver actions and native controls.
