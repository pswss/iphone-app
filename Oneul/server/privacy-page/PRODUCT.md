# Oneul Website Product Context

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are Korean iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and Mac users who want a calmer way to see and enter their daily schedule. They may arrive from an App Store listing, a shared link, or search while deciding whether Oneul fits their devices.

## Product Purpose

Explain Oneul in seconds, show the real product across Apple devices, establish privacy trust, and send Mac visitors to the native Mac download when a signed public build exists. Success means visitors understand the daily-timeline model and can find the right platform action without guessing.

## Positioning

Oneul turns everyday Korean into a clear, private timeline for today.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: download Oneul for Mac when a signed public build is available.
- Secondary CTA: explore core features and supported devices.
- Ten-second memory: "Say it. See your day."
- Belief ladder: Oneul makes the day understandable; natural-language entry removes friction; the same day stays visible across Apple devices; original schedules stay in the user's private stores while Live Activity display data is retained only briefly.
- Proof on hand: current Oneul product screenshots, native iPhone/iPad/Mac/Watch targets, on-device schedule parsing, private CloudKit storage, a three-day maximum for Live Activity relay data, no ads, and no analytics. No testimonials or usage numbers are available, so none may be invented.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, and native. The page should feel like a quiet Apple-platform utility with a distinct rainbow timeline, not a generic SaaS campaign.

## Anti-references

Avoid copying any competitor's trade dress, long encyclopedia-style feature pages, testimonial theater, repeated glass cards, abstract AI gradients, fake statistics, and automatic OS redirects. Reference Fantastical for clear Mac download intent, Structured for cross-device continuity, and Things for concise information architecture only.

## Design Principles

- Show the real timeline before explaining it.
- Adapt the primary CTA, never replace or redirect the whole page based on device.
- Keep one dominant promise and three supporting product stories.
- Use privacy specifics as product proof, not vague trust language.
- Preserve user agency with visible device options and accessible, reduced-motion behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keep keyboard focus visible, controls at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, meaningful alt text, semantic headings, strong contrast, and complete reduced-motion, reduced-transparency, dark-mode, and increased-contrast fallbacks.
